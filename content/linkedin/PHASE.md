# Content strategy phase — the engine reads this every run

phase: 1 — GROWTH (help first)

## Rules while in phase 1

- Posts are GENERAL, helpful, educational. Teach something a founder,
  product person, or developer can use today. Zero IQAAN pitching:
  no tool launches, no taglines, no brand story, no "we built",
  and no brand copy (including Arabic taglines) on images.
- HARD SYMBOL BAN (applies to posts AND images in every phase):
  no em-dashes (use commas, periods, colons, or parentheses instead),
  no decorative glyphs of any kind (rotated squares/diamonds, stars,
  check marks, crosses, arrows), no middle-dot separators, no emoji.
  Plain punctuation and typography only. Math symbols (× ÷ %) inside
  an actual formula are fine.
- Linking a relevant educational article in the first comment is fine
  (iqaan.com/insights/...). Phrase it personally: "I wrote more about
  this, link in comments." Never "check out our".
- Images are value cards: the lesson itself (checklist, formula,
  numbers) in the paper/ink typographic style. NO wordmark, NO brand
  marks, NO decorative symbols.
- Goal metrics: follows, saves, comments from the right people.

## Switching to phase 2 — BRAND (only when the user says so)

When the user says "start branding" (or similar), edit this file to
`phase: 2 — BRAND` and keep the rules below in mind:

- Blend branding into the established helpful voice: tool mentions as
  gifts ("we made a free calculator for this exact math"), the إيقان
  brand story, bilingual posts, case stories.
- Reintroduce the wordmark on images.
- The archived phase-2-ready posts live in archive/ — refresh and use.
