Nobody can quote your software project from a paragraph.

But the shapes are knowable — and knowing them before the sales calls is a superpower.

The honest ranges for a competent team in 2026:

• Focused web MVP: $25–50k
• Typical polished product: $60–150k
• Complex platform: $150–400k+
• Mobile adds 20–40% over web
• Regulated domains multiply by 1.5× or more

Then the part nobody puts on the first page of a proposal — the second invoice:

Maintenance runs 15–20% of the build cost, every year. Forever.

So a "$100k" product is really a $100k build plus a $15–20k/year subscription to keeping it alive.

None of these numbers are secrets. They're just rarely said out loud before a contract is on the table.

Three red flags when you get a quote:

1. No itemization — a number without breakdowns is a feeling with a dollar sign
2. Exactly round numbers — real estimates end in strange digits
3. No exclusions section — migration, load testing, and app-store fees are where budgets go to die
