A client once got quoted $300k for what should have been a $60k build. They showed me the proposal. No breakdowns anywhere. Just the number.

I've thought about that document a lot.

So here are the ranges I'd give a friend over coffee, no NDA required:

A focused web MVP sits around $25-50k. A real product with proper design and an admin side, more like $60-150k. Complex platforms start at $150k and keep climbing. Mobile adds maybe a quarter over web. Regulated field? Multiply everything by 1.5.

Here's the line item almost nobody mentions though. Keeping software alive costs 15-20% of the build price, every year, forever. That "affordable" $40k app is really a $40k app plus $6-8k a year.

When a quote lands on your desk, check three things.

That it's itemized. That the total isn't suspiciously round, because real math ends in ugly digits. And that someone wrote down what's NOT included, since migration and load testing are where budgets quietly die.

We put the full breakdown in an article, and built a calculator so you can plug in your own scope and see where you land. Link in comments.
