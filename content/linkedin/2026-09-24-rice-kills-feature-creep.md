Every feature in your backlog sounds essential. That's exactly the problem.

In a room full of smart people, everything gets defended. "Users will expect it." "Competitors have it." Try arguing with that in a meeting. You can't, because it's not an argument, it's a vibe.

So stop arguing and start scoring. The method I reach for is RICE, and it's stupidly simple.

Score each feature on four things: Reach (how many users touch it), Impact (how much it moves them), Confidence (how sure you are), and Effort (person-months to build). Then multiply the first three and divide by effort.

Watch what happens with four typical features:

User accounts: reach 10, impact 3, confidence high, effort 2. Score 15.
Email notifications: reach 8, impact 1, confidence medium, effort 1. Score 5.6.
Payments: reach 3, impact 3, confidence medium, effort 3. Score 2.4.
Admin dashboard: reach 1, impact 2, confidence high, effort 2. Score 0.9.

Payments losing to notifications upsets people the first time they see it. Then it clicks: payments serves users you don't have yet. Notifications serves the ones you do.

The numbers aren't magic. Their job is forcing opinions into public, where they can be argued with. A meeting can't beat arithmetic, but arithmetic can settle a meeting.

Draw one line under the sorted list. Above ships. Below waits. Then protect that line like it's the budget, because it is.
