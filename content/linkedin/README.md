# IQAAN LinkedIn Content Engine

Drafts land here automatically every **Sunday and Wednesday, 9:00am**.
Each file is one ready-to-post draft. Approve at a glance → copy → paste to LinkedIn.

## How to publish (2 minutes)

1. Post from your **personal profile** (LinkedIn gives profiles the reach, pages get suppressed) and **tag the IQAAN company page** in the first line.
2. Paste the post body. Put the **link in the first comment** — LinkedIn deprioritizes posts with external links in the body.
3. Best windows: Tue–Thu 8–10am, or Sun evening. The draft headers include a specific tip per post.

## Voice rules (what the engine writes to)

- Confident, spare, editorial. One idea per post. Numbers in hooks.
- No fluff, no "unlock/delve/landscape", no emoji walls.
- Bilingual EN+AR roughly every other post — it differentiates in both feeds.
- Substance comes from the real site: /tools calculators, /insights essays, the إيقان brand story.

## Topic rotation

(a) micro-lesson · (b) tool lead-magnet · (c) brand story · (d) industry opinion — no topic repeats within 4 weeks.

## Feedback loop

Monthly: paste your LinkedIn analytics numbers into this folder as `stats-YYYY-MM.md` and the engine reads them before writing.
